About

Supporting learners with patience, care, and creativity.

I’m Fatima Grace C. Gonzaga, a Filipino education graduate with experience in classroom teaching, tutorial coordination, and one-to-one tutoring. I bring patience, adaptability, and clear communication to the work of helping learners build skills and confidence.

Professional Profile

As a magna cum laude Bachelor of Secondary Education graduate majoring in Filipino, I have supported learners in school, tutorial-center, and individual tutoring settings. My experience includes Filipino instruction, learner-progress monitoring, basic lesson planning, classroom management, and communication with families and fellow educators.
